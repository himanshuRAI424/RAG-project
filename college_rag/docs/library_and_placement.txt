Greenfield Institute of Technology - Library and Placement Guidelines
Last updated: 2025-04-20

Library
The central library is open from 8 AM to 8 PM on working days. Each student may borrow up to three books at a time. The loan period is 14 days. A fine of Rs 2 per day is charged for every day a book is returned late. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current market price.

Placement
The Training and Placement Cell conducts campus drives from August to March. To be eligible for campus recruitment a student must have a minimum CGPA of 6.0 and no active backlogs. Students who receive an offer from a Tier-1 company are not allowed to sit for further drives unless the offer is a dream offer approved by the cell.
