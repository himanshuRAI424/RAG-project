Greenfield Institute of Technology - Hostel Rules and Regulations
Last updated: 2025-05-15

1. Hostel Fee
The hostel fee is Rs 60,000 per academic year for a shared double room. Mess charges are Rs 3,000 per month and are billed separately.

2. Timings
The hostel gate closes at 10 PM every day. Students returning after 10 PM must make an entry in the late register and inform the warden. Repeated late entry may lead to a fine.

3. Visitors
Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' lounge between 4 PM and 6 PM. Parents may stay overnight with prior written permission from the warden.

4. Mess Timings
Breakfast is served from 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM and dinner from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM.
