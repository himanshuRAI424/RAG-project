Greenfield Institute of Technology - Examination Rules Circular
Last updated: 2025-01-10

1. Eligibility
As per the new examination policy, students must have a minimum of 80% attendance in each subject to be allowed to sit for the end semester examination. This circular applies from the even semester of 2025.

2. Examination Hall Rules
Students must carry their college ID card and hall ticket. Mobile phones, smart watches and any electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall. Students must reach the hall 20 minutes before the start time.

3. Re-evaluation
A student may apply for re-evaluation within 7 days of result declaration. The re-evaluation fee is Rs 500 per paper. The revised marks are final.

4. Malpractice
Any student found using unfair means will be debarred from the examination and the case will be referred to the discipline committee.
