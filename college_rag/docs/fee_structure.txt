Greenfield Institute of Technology - Fee Structure 2025-26
Last updated: 2025-06-01

1. Tuition Fee
The tuition fee for the B.Tech programme is Rs 1,20,000 per academic year. The tuition fee for the M.Tech programme is Rs 90,000 per academic year.

2. Payment Deadline
All fees, including tuition fee and hostel fee, must be paid on or before 15 August of the academic year. A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged after the due date.

3. Mode of Payment
Fees can be paid online through the student portal using UPI, net banking or debit card. Cash payments are accepted at the accounts office between 10 AM and 3 PM on working days.

4. Refund
If a student withdraws before the start of the semester, 90% of the tuition fee is refunded. No refund is given after the semester begins.
