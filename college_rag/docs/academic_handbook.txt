Greenfield Institute of Technology - Academic Handbook
Last updated: 2024-07-01

1. Attendance
Every student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject to be eligible to appear in the end semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a medical certificate.

2. Grading
The institute follows a 10-point CGPA system. A grade of O corresponds to 10 points, A+ to 9 points, A to 8 points, B+ to 7 points and B to 6 points. A student must score at least 40% marks in each subject to pass.

3. Backlogs
A student may carry a maximum of four backlog subjects into the next academic year. Backlog exams are conducted twice a year, in December and in May.

4. Academic Calendar
The odd semester begins in the first week of July and the even semester begins in the first week of January. Mid-semester tests are held in the eighth week of each semester.
